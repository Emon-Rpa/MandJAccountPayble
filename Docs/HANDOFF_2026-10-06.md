## DB findings (real mailbox and statement)
- The statement has four account blocks starting at `B/F` rows (rows 3, 36, 122, 157 of `DHAKABANK.xlsx`). Account number and currency are not in the sheet. For the USD block the advice shows account `2251300000074`.
- Advice types: IBCPA (import bill payment), IBCA (acceptance), AA (LC issue), SA (shipping guarantee), LC (export LC). Each PDF has a table `DESCRIPTION DR/CR CCY AMOUNT A/C NO` with one or more debit lines. Header fields differ by type. `OUR REF NO` in IBCPA/IBCA equals the statement reference; SA uses `...G01`; the AA match for row 146 is via the LC number.
- Subject and body only carry the contract number (e.g. `225BC04260210505`), never the statement reference, so the PDF text is the only place to match.
- Matching by reference: 16 rows. Matching by amount adds none, so it was not added (false-positive risk). 78 debit rows have no advice mail at all: internal fund transfers, BG collateral/stamp/commission/VAT, cheque clearing, cash deposit charges, processing fees, loan principal repayments, RTGS/TT, bank charges. Those correctly end as "Mail not found".
- Mailbox `rpa_center` only has mails for roughly 20 Feb to Apr 2026 in the files checked; the Feb 5-18 import bill rows were outside my downloaded window, so they were not verified.
